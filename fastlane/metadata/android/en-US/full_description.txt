Šiukšliavežė reminds you the evening before the garbage truck comes, so you never miss a pickup again. It covers the villages and towns of 33 municipalities, with the schedules their waste companies publish:

• Vilnius district: UAB „Nemenčinės komunalininkas“ and UAB „Nemėžio komunalininkas“.
• Kaunas and Kaišiadorys districts: UAB „Kauno švara“, by address. For glass and packaging the app links to the schedules of UAB „Ekonovus“.
• Mažeikiai, Telšiai and Plungė districts and Rietavas municipality: UAB „Telšių regiono atliekų tvarkymo centras“.
• Klaipėda district: VšĮ „Gargždų švara“.
• Jurbarkas, Tauragė and Šilalė districts and Pagėgiai municipality: UAB „Tauragės regiono atliekų tvarkymo centras“.
• Šiauliai, Akmenė, Joniškis, Kelmė, Pakruojis and Radviliškis districts: UAB „Šiaulių regiono atliekų tvarkymo centras“.
• Alytus, Prienai, Lazdijai and Varėna districts and Druskininkai and Birštonas municipalities: UAB „Alytaus regiono atliekų tvarkymo centras“.
• Biržai, Pasvalys and Rokiškis districts, and only packaging and glass in Panevėžys district: UAB „Panevėžio regiono atliekų tvarkymo centras“.
• Marijampolė, Kalvarija and Kazlų Rūda municipalities and Vilkaviškis and Šakiai districts: UAB „Marijampolės apskrities atliekų tvarkymo centras“.

Mixed waste, packaging and glass, and food and green waste where they're collected separately. The cities of Vilnius, Kaunas, Klaipėda, Šiauliai, Panevėžys and Alytus aren't covered.

How it works
• Choose your village or town once, or let the app find your address. If pickup days in your place depend on the street or the kind of home, it asks about that too.
• See your pickups as a list or a month calendar, or add them to your calendar app.
• A home screen widget shows the next pickup.
• Reminders the evening before, that morning or both, at any time you choose. Glass comes rarely? Give it an extra reminder. Mark “Bins are out” or snooze right from the reminder.
• New schedules load automatically, and you hear right away when a pickup date changes.
• Save more than one place, like a summer house, and get reminders for each.
• Place not listed, or your bin is emptied on other days? Set your own schedule, every 1–4 weeks or every 1–3 months, and skip or move single dates around holidays.
• No glass or packaging container? Turn off its reminders.
• Not sure which bin something goes in? Look it up in the sorting guide.
• In Lithuanian, English, Russian and Polish.

Private by design
• No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Everything you choose stays on your phone, and the phone schedules the reminders itself.
• "Use my current location" works on the phone; your location is never sent anywhere.

Free and open source
The app is free, and its source code is open (Apache 2.0) on GitHub: github.com/AurimasNav/atlieku-surinkimo-grafikas. Wrong dates or a missing address? Report it right from the app.

The schedules are checked every day. Settlement and street names come from the Address Register of VĮ Registrų centras (open data).

Unofficial app made by volunteers, not affiliated with the companies and organisations named here or with the companies that collect the waste. Dates come from the schedules they publish and may contain mistakes; always check the official schedule if in doubt. Provided free of charge, without any warranty.
