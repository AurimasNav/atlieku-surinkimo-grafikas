Šiukšliavežė reminds you the evening before the garbage truck comes, so you never miss a pickup again. It covers two districts.

• Vilnius district: mixed waste, packaging and glass in more than 1,000 villages and towns served by UAB „Nemenčinės komunalininkas“ and UAB „Nemėžio komunalininkas“, including Nemenčinė, Riešė, Avižieniai, Maišiagala, Nemėžis, Pagiriai, Juodšiliai, Mickūnai and Rudamina.
• Kaunas district: mixed, food and green waste at about 39,000 addresses served by UAB „Kauno švara“, including Garliava, Domeikava, Ringaudai, Raudondvaris, Užliedžiai and Akademija.

How it works
• Choose your village or town once, or let the app find your address. If pickup days in your place depend on the street or the kind of home, it asks about that too.
• See all your pickups in one place: the next one, how many days are left and the dates after that.
• Get a reminder the evening before or that morning, at a time you choose.
• New schedules load automatically, so your reminders stay up to date.
• Place not listed, or your bin is emptied on other days? Set your own schedule: every 1–4 weeks or every 1–3 months.
• No glass or packaging container? Turn off its reminders.
• In Lithuanian, English, Russian and Polish.

Private by design
• No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Everything you choose stays on your phone, and the phone schedules the reminders itself.
• "Use my current location" works on the phone; your location is never sent anywhere.

Free and open source
The app is free, and its source code is open (Apache 2.0) on GitHub: github.com/AurimasNav/atlieku-surinkimo-grafikas. Wrong dates or a missing address? Report it right from the app.

Schedules come from the files published at nemenkom.lt and nkom.lt and from the open data of UAB „Kauno švara“, all checked every day. Settlement and street names come from the Address Register of VĮ Registrų centras (open data).

Unofficial app made by volunteers, not affiliated with UAB „Nemenčinės komunalininkas“, UAB „Nemėžio komunalininkas“ or UAB „Kauno švara“. Dates come from the schedules they publish and may contain mistakes; always check the official schedule if in doubt. Provided free of charge, without any warranty.
