Šiukšliavežė reminds you the evening before the garbage truck comes, so you never miss a pickup again. It covers four districts.

• Vilnius district: mixed waste, packaging and glass in more than 1,000 villages and towns served by UAB „Nemenčinės komunalininkas“ and UAB „Nemėžio komunalininkas“, including Nemenčinė, Riešė, Avižieniai, Maišiagala, Nemėžis, Pagiriai, Juodšiliai, Mickūnai and Rudamina.
• Kaunas district: mixed, food and green waste at about 39,000 addresses served by UAB „Kauno švara“, including Garliava, Domeikava, Ringaudai, Raudondvaris, Užliedžiai and Akademija. Glass and packaging there are collected by UAB „Ekonovus“; the app links to its schedules.
• Mažeikiai district: mixed waste, packaging and glass in Mažeikiai, Viekšniai, Seda, Tirkšliai, Židikai and about 150 villages, plus food waste in Mažeikiai town. The schedules are published by UAB „Telšių regiono atliekų tvarkymo centras“; the waste is collected by UAB „Ecoservice“ and UAB „Virginijus ir Ko“.
• Klaipėda district: mixed waste, packaging, glass and green waste in Gargždai, Priekulė, Dovilai, Kretingalė, Vėžaičiai and about 270 villages. The schedules are published by VšĮ „Gargždų švara“; the waste is collected by UAB „Ekonovus“.

How it works
• Choose your village or town once, or let the app find your address. If pickup days in your place depend on the street or the kind of home, it asks about that too.
• See your pickups as a list or a month calendar, or add them to your calendar app.
• A home screen widget shows the next pickup.
• Reminders the evening before, that morning or both, at any time you choose. Glass comes rarely? Give it an extra reminder. Mark “Bins are out” or snooze right from the reminder.
• New schedules load automatically, and you hear right away when a pickup date changes.
• Save more than one place, like a summer house, and get reminders for each.
• Place not listed, or your bin is emptied on other days? Set your own schedule, every 1–4 weeks or every 1–3 months, and skip or move single dates around holidays.
• No glass or packaging container? Turn off its reminders.
• Not sure which bin something goes in? Look it up in the sorting guide.
• In Lithuanian, English, Russian and Polish.

Private by design
• No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Everything you choose stays on your phone, and the phone schedules the reminders itself.
• "Use my current location" works on the phone; your location is never sent anywhere.

Free and open source
The app is free, and its source code is open (Apache 2.0) on GitHub: github.com/AurimasNav/atlieku-surinkimo-grafikas. Wrong dates or a missing address? Report it right from the app.

Schedules come from the files published at nemenkom.lt, nkom.lt, tratc.lt and gargzdusvara.eu and from the open data of UAB „Kauno švara“, all checked every day. Settlement and street names come from the Address Register of VĮ Registrų centras (open data).
